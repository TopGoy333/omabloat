omabloat

Find bloat on Omarchy — read-only — plus safe cleanup and Steam setup.

find-omarchy-bloat.sh detects bloat without changing anything. debloat-omarchy.sh removes only what you confirm.

Quick start

chmod +x find-omarchy-bloat.sh debloat-omarchy.sh
./find-omarchy-bloat.sh
./debloat-omarchy.sh --audit

Bar button

omarchy plugin add https://github.com/TopGoy333/omabloat --enable

Installs topgoy333.omabloat from the bar-plugin/ folder.

Safety

  • The detector is read-only.
  • Cleanup asks first and never runs freely.
  • Dolphin, Bazaar, Omarchy, and claude-* are guarded.
